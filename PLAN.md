# Reef Relay plan

Build a static, offline canvas game about weaving gentle currents to deliver luminous spores to their matching coral beds. The first tide teaches the loop with one obvious vane and a nearby garden; later tides add route choices, cross-currents, and a maelstrom while keeping previews and timing generous.

The shell frames a large illustrated reef with compact tide, score, streak, oxygen, and control indicators. Click a vane or select it with arrows/WASD and rotate with Space/Enter; pulse reroutes a spore in danger. Start, pause, mute, help, restart, clear win and oxygen-loss states remain available to mouse and keyboard users. Art is procedural canvas drawing, sound is synthesized locally after input, and all runtime files are local for file:// support.

Review against the brief: the first delivery should happen quickly and visibly; route choices need a legible next-vector preview and matching color/shape cues; failure must follow a generous grace window; five short tides provide escalation and a clear win; screenshots must show title, live play, and a result, rendered by Chromium from the finished game. The game stays small, dependency-free, and split by job.
